How it works

Unify → contextualize → answer

The concept in three moves — the same shape as the real platform, here re-created end-to-end in ~700 lines of dependency-free JavaScript.

01 · UNIFY

One layer over all the data

Clubs drown in event data, tracking feeds, video, contracts and scouting notes spread across vendors. The layer's job is to sit on top of all of it. Here: a global attribute baseline for 16,161 real players, joined to three seasons of FBref match data so ratings are derived from observed output wherever public event data exists.

02 · CONTEXTUALIZE

Scored against your DNA

The same player is a great signing for one club and a mistake for another. Every score here is computed against a club profile — game model, budget, current squad — and re-ranks live when you change it in the sidebar.

03 · ANSWER

Prose, with the reasoning shown

Ask in plain language; get shortlists, 360° reports and market alerts back as text — and every number expands to show exactly which factors produced it. Explainability is the product.

Architecture

Nine models, one answer

The real platform runs nine proprietary AI/ML models (players as high-dimensional vectors, transformers over match sequences). This clone mirrors the structure with nine transparent scoring modules — eight score, the ninth writes. They are arithmetic over real data, not learned models: the honesty is the point.

Workflows

What a recruitment team actually does with it

Shortlist building

"Young left-footed winger with dribbling" → ranked real candidates with fit scores and the constraints the parser understood, spelled out.

360° scout reports

Any player, full picture in seconds: performance, physical, trajectory, synergy vs. your squad — ending in a pursue / monitor / pass verdict.

Squad planning

Pick any of 638 real clubs as "your" squad, then ask where it's weakest — a line-by-line quality and age audit with succession flags.

Market watch

A scan over real three-season trends for collapsing minutes, rising output and genuine breakout seasons — no invented valuations.

Similarity search

"Find players similar to X" — nearest neighbours in attribute-vector space, the demo's stand-in for learned player embeddings.

Honest confidence

Every player is badged verified, partial or baseline by how much real match data backs their rating — and every score expands to show its factors.
