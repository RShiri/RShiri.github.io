Odds ↔ probability — the arithmetic you must do instantly

Decimal odds are the payout per 1 unit staked. Implied probability = 1 / decimal odds.

Decimal oddsImplied probabilityMeaning
1.5066.7%heavy favourite
2.0050%even money
2.5040%slight underdog
4.0025%outsider

Reverse: fair odds = 1 / probability. Your model says home win = 62% → fair price 1.61.

Overround — the bookmaker's margin

Sum the implied probabilities of all outcomes of a market. A fair book sums to 100%; a real book sums to more — the excess is the overround (a.k.a. vig, juice, margin).

1X2 priced at:  home 2.10   draw 3.40   away 3.80
implied:        47.6%     + 29.4%     + 26.3%    = 103.3%
overround = 3.3%  → whatever happens, the book keeps an edge.

The market types beyond 1X2

Over/Under (totals)

Bet on total goals vs a line, teams irrelevant. Over 2.5 wins on 3+ goals, loses on 0–2. Half-lines (2.5) can't tie. Whole lines (2.0, 3.0) can land exactly → push: stake refunded. Your Poisson score grid prices this directly: P(over 2.5) = sum of grid cells where home+away ≥ 3.

Asian handicap

A virtual head start eliminates (or reduces) the draw. Barcelona −1.5 means Barcelona must win by 2+. Lines:

  • Half lines (−0.5, −1.5): two outcomes only, no push possible.
  • Whole lines (−1.0): win by exactly 1 → push, stake refunded.
  • Quarter lines (−0.75 = "−0.5 & −1.0"): stake split half on each line. Win by 1 → half wins (−0.5 side), half pushes (−1.0 side) → "half win."

Others you should recognize

Correct score (exact final score — literally one cell of your Poisson grid), BTTS (both teams to score), Double chance (1X, X2, 12), Draw no bet (= handicap 0: draw refunds). In-play versions of everything, with markets suspended (bets rejected) during goals/VAR while prices reset.

Settlement — where the money moves

Settlement = officially deciding each market from the result (your MsgType 35). The edge cases are where feed companies earn their keep:

  • VAR-cancelled goal: a goal was fed, markets settled or repriced, then the goal is disallowed. The feed must issue corrections; bets settled on the phantom goal get re-settled. This is why "goal confirmed" vs "goal scored" states exist.
  • Abandoned match: house rules apply — typically markets already decided (first goalscorer, HT result if the half finished) stand; undecided markets are voided (stakes refunded).
  • 90 minutes vs extra time: most football markets settle on regular time (+ injury time) only. A cup match won in extra time still settles the 1X2 as a draw. Feeds carry period-scoped scores for this.
  • Push: not a win, not a loss — stake returned (whole-line handicaps and totals).
