# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Hiring managers in sports-data companies** (heads of data, CTOs, senior data engineers at companies like Marquee and LSports). They open a project, read code, and probe depth. They need to see real, working sports-data products and the engineering behind them.
- **Recruiters and HR screeners.** They scan in seconds for role fit, availability, core skills and a CV. They need the headline facts and a CV download without hunting.

The top of the page serves the screener; scrolling serves the hiring manager.

## Product Purpose

Ram Shiri's personal portfolio. It exists to get him a student or full-time role in data engineering / AI in the sports-data industry. Success is an employer reaching out, or a hiring manager opening a live project and staying.

## Positioning

A third-year Data Engineering student who is in love with the meeting point of data and sport, and proves it by shipping: many live, working sports-analytics products built on his own time (xG models, match centres, live pipelines, dashboards). The pitch is not the longest skills list; it is "give me anything in this field and I will learn it and do it properly", backed by the volume and finish of what is already live.

## Operating Context

- Static site on GitHub Pages (`rshiri.github.io`), no build step; plain HTML/CSS/JS.
- Visitors arrive from a CV link, LinkedIn, or a direct message, often on a phone.
- Projects live on sibling GitHub Pages sites (`/BCNFINAL/`, `/XEPL/`, `/XLALIGA/`, `/F1Visualized/`, `/XWORLDCUPTWIT/...`) and in sub-folders of this repo (`xg-model/`, `winprob-model/`, `live-pipeline/`). One project (NBA dashboard) is a Streamlit app that sleeps when idle.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive analytics embedded on the homepage (Lamine Yamal xG shot map and take-on map, Argentina WC2026 match centre with goal replay and win-probability, F1 race progression) are kept and featured.
- Main projects: NBA dashboard, World Cup 2026 dashboard, F1 Visualized, XEPL, XLALIGA.
- Smaller projects (shown compactly, not prominent): FC Barcelona xG Lab, the xG model explainer, the win-probability model write-up, the live match pipeline (MatchFeed).
- Light/dark theme toggle with the `theme` localStorage key is shared with sub-pages.
- Must work at phone width and stay fast on GitHub Pages (no framework).
- Private study material (CV bootcamp, LSports prep) does not belong on the public site.

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't claim: every capability on the page links to something that runs.
2. Ten seconds for the screener, ten minutes for the engineer.
3. Passion is the differentiator, and the proof is volume and finish of shipped work.
4. Honest over impressive: nothing on the page that can't be defended in an interview.
